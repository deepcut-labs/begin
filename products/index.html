Two Mac apps

Both are native, sandboxed and built for macOS 26. Both read your own files and keep the results on your Mac.

Agent Configurator

In beta · macOS 26 or later · Apple Silicon

One library for the rules, skills, commands, context files and subagent definitions your AI coding agents run on — written once, deployed into any project, in the format each runtime expects. It also reads the session transcripts Claude Code and Codex CLI already write to your Mac, and turns them into readable activity, search, tool and cost reporting.

MDview

Free · macOS 26 or later

A Quick Look preview extension for Markdown. Select a .md file in Finder, press the spacebar, and read it properly rendered — GitHub-flavoured tables, task lists, strikethrough, autolinks and footnotes — styled with a stylesheet you supply, in light or dark on your terms.

What they have in common

Both apps are written in Swift, run sandboxed with least-privilege entitlements, and follow the Apple Human Interface Guidelines — light and dark appearance, full keyboard and menu-bar coverage, and VoiceOver support on every control.

Neither app contains analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or an advertising identifier. What each one does and does not touch is set out in our privacy policy, product by product.
