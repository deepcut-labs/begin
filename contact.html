Reporting a bug

A useful bug report tells us three things: what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. If you can add the app version — it's in the About window, under the app menu — and the version of macOS you're running, we can usually reproduce it on the first try.

If you're testing a beta through TestFlight, sending feedback from within TestFlight attaches a screenshot and the system details automatically, which saves you the trouble.
