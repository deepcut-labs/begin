Support and feedback

Beta testers
While an app is in beta, send feedback from within TestFlight. It attaches a screenshot and the system details automatically, and it reaches us directly.
App Store
Once an app ships, the support link on its App Store page brings you back here, and reviews and reports left there reach us too.
Privacy
Questions about our privacy policy or a data request can be raised through either channel above.

Reporting a bug

A useful bug report tells us three things: what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. If you can add the app version — it's in the About window, under the app menu — and the version of macOS you're running, we can usually reproduce it on the first try.
