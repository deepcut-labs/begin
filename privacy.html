Privacy Policy

Agent Configurator · Deepcut Labs, LLC · Last updated 13 August 2026

Agent Configurator does not have a server. We operate no backend, no account system, and no analytics. We do not receive your data, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app creates stays on your Mac, under your control:

  • Your library — rules, skills, commands, agents, context and profiles — is a folder of ordinary files. It lives in your Documents folder by default, or in your own iCloud Drive if you choose that, or in any folder you pick.
  • Settings and app state live in the app's sandbox container on your Mac.
  • API keys and repository access tokens are stored in your macOS Keychain. They never leave your Mac except as authentication to the service you entered them for.

What the app reads

Agent Configurator reads AI coding-agent configuration and session data already on your Mac — files such as ~/.claude, ~/.cursor and the projects you register. It reads these only from folders you explicitly grant access to through the macOS folder picker, and it reads them locally to show you your own usage, costs and configuration. That information is never transmitted anywhere.

When the app uses the network

Only when you ask it to, and only to services you have configured yourself:

  • AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI) — when you use the assisted authoring features. Requests go directly from your Mac to that provider, authenticated with your own API key, and are governed by that provider's privacy policy.
  • Repository and content sources (GitHub and other git hosts, Context7) — when you add a remote source and it syncs. Requests go directly to that service using credentials you supplied.

There is no telemetry, no crash reporting, no usage tracking, no advertising identifier, and no third-party analytics SDK of any kind. The app does not phone home, and it does not check in with us on launch or at any other time.

What we collect

Nothing. Deepcut Labs receives no personal information, no usage data, no file contents and no diagnostics from the app. If you contact us by email, we will of course have whatever you put in that email, and we use it only to reply to you.

Beta testing

While the app is distributed through TestFlight, Apple collects installation and crash information under its own terms. That is Apple's collection, not ours — see Apple's Privacy Policy. We may see aggregate tester counts and crash reports that Apple provides. If you send feedback through TestFlight, we receive what you write and any screenshot you attach.

Children

Agent Configurator is a developer tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, we will say so here before it ships.
