What we care about

Every app we ship is native, sandboxed, and built to the Apple Human Interface Guidelines — full light and dark appearance, complete keyboard and menu-bar coverage, and VoiceOver support throughout.

We do not run analytics, and we do not collect your data. Our apps read what is already on your Mac and keep it there. When one of them reaches the network, it is because you asked it to, and it goes to a service you configured yourself. That is described in full in our privacy policy.
